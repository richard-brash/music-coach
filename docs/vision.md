# Vision

This project will become an AI-powered offline music coach that helps musicians practice with focused, private, and resilient guidance without requiring an internet connection during sessions.

## Product intent

- Help learners improve timing, pitch, tone, technique, ear training, and repertoire through personalized feedback.
- Support practice anywhere, including studios, classrooms, rehearsal rooms, and low-connectivity environments.
- Keep recorded audio, practice notes, and progress history local-first so musicians remain in control of their data.
- Provide encouraging coaching that adapts to each musician's instrument, skill level, goals, and practice habits.

## Guiding principles

- Offline-first: core coaching, analysis, and practice history should work without network access.
- Musician-centered: feedback should be actionable, specific, and motivating rather than overwhelming.
- Privacy-preserving: audio and performance data should stay on the user's device by default.
- Practice-focused: every feature should help users spend more time playing and less time configuring tools.
